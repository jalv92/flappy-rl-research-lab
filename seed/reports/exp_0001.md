## Hypothesis
- Area: loss_and_update_rules
- Motivation: Hard target copies every 1000 steps change the regression target abruptly; collapses often appear right after the policy first becomes good.
- Modification: Replace periodic hard target syncs with per-update Polyak averaging (tau=0.005).
- Expected benefit: Fewer degradations and lower between-seed variance of the final policy.
- Failure modes considered: Slower target tracking may delay early learning (lower AUC).
- Prior work: Lillicrap et al. 2016 (DDPG soft updates).
- Novelty status: known
- Baseline: xdqn control: identical hyperparameters, step budget, seeds and evaluation schedule
- Criteria: Primary: validation AUC (sample efficiency) and final-policy validation mean, per training seed, candidate vs control. Promising only if a 95% bootstrap CI of the difference excludes 0 in the favourable direction and no primary metric is significantly worse. Secondary: degradation count, seed CV, wall-clock ratio.

## Setup
